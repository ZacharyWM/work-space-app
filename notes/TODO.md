## User feedback

The air lock could let you leave one ship and travel to another workspace you have access to. If more teams onboarded, that would make a "fleet". Popping open the existing list of ships you have access to is probably enough.

Slack integrations would be great (no one knows what that would look like yet).

With the page open, the computer draws about 4W more power (baseline 6 to 10W, rising to 10 to 15W).
