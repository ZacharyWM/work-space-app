# Screen sharing

We need a screen sharing feature to share your screen with people in the same zone. I think we'll also need a new "meeting mode" where when you are in a zone with other people, you can switch from seeing the workspace/spaceship area to seeing the cameras of everyone who is in the zone. When in meeting mode, screen sharing will mostly take up the whole view. You can make whatever decisions you think best for the user experience.

The ClaudeDesignPrototype files don't have prototypes for these features, so you can design it however you think best.

Put all your decisions and progress in the @notes/003-screen-sharing/progress.md file.
